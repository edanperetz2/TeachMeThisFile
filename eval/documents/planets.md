# The Planets of the Solar System

Eight planets orbit the Sun. The four inner planets (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars) are small and rocky. The four outer planets are much larger and are made mostly of gas or ice.

| Planet | Distance from Sun (million km) | Moons | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mercury | 58 | 0 | Rocky |
| Venus | 108 | 0 | Rocky |
| Earth | 150 | 1 | Rocky |
| Mars | 228 | 2 | Rocky |
| Jupiter | 778 | 95 | Gas giant |
| Saturn | 1,434 | 146 | Gas giant |
| Uranus | 2,871 | 28 | Ice giant |
| Neptune | 4,495 | 16 | Ice giant |

The further a planet is from the Sun, the longer its year. Jupiter and Saturn have the most moons.
